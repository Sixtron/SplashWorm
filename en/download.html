System requirements

Operating systemWindows 10 or newer, 64-bit
Memory4 GB
GraphicsAny card that supports DirectX 11
InternetRequired at all times; the game only runs online

How to install it

  1. Download the ZIP file.
  2. Right-click the file, then "Extract all" into a folder of its own.
  3. In the extracted folder, start SplashWorm.exe.

On first start Windows may show a warning because the game has no paid signature. Choose "More info" and then "Run anyway" to continue.

Controls on the PC

The worm follows your mouse pointer, and you have four buttons whose keys you can reassign in the settings. Everything else is in the guide.
