This policy covers the game SplashWorm (Android app and Windows version) and this website. SplashWorm is a private, non-commercial project and is offered free of charge.

The short version

There is no advertising, no purchases and no analytics or tracking in the game. We only store what is needed for your player account and the leaderboard. Data is never sold and never shared with third parties for advertising.

2. This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your device transmits technically necessary data to GitHub, in particular your IP address, date and time, the address requested, and details about your browser and operating system. GitHub stores this access data in server logs in order to deliver and secure the service. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). We have no access to these logs and produce no statistics from them. Transfer to the USA is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics services and loads no external fonts or scripts.

3. Connection to the game server

The game only works online. Your device connects to our game server, which is operated on Oracle Cloud. In doing so your IP address and the live game data (position, length, score of the current round) are processed. This is technically necessary for the game to work. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Your IP address is only used for the duration of the connection and is not stored permanently with your account.

Your device also reports technical performance values to the server: frame rate, longest frame time, screen size and the chosen quality level. This tells us whether the game stutters on particular devices so that we can improve it. These values appear in the server log and are not stored with your account. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a smoothly running game).

4. Signing in with Google

You can sign in with a Google account so that your progress is kept when you change devices. From Google we receive:

  • an account identifier (a number we use to recognise your account),
  • your display name,
  • your e-mail address.

We receive no access to your Google password, your contacts, your e-mails or your files. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The sign-in itself is provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. See policies.google.com/privacy

Sign-in data is transmitted encrypted. The proof of identity from Google, which contains your e-mail address, is encrypted on your device before it is sent, and only our game server can decrypt it. It therefore cannot be read by others on the same network – in an open Wi-Fi, for example – and an intercepted sign-in message cannot be replayed to impersonate you. The live game data described in section 3 (position, length, score) is not encrypted; apart from the player name you choose yourself, which is shown to everyone you play with, it contains no personal data.

5. What is stored on the game server

We store exactly the following for your account:

Account identifierThe identifier from Google, so we can find your account.
Player nameThe name shown above your worm in the game.
E-mail addressUsed only for one reminder before a long-unused account is deleted (see section 8). No newsletter and no advertising is ever sent.
HeadshotsYour number of headshots. The leaderboard is ranked by this.
ScoreThe points you have collected.
Best roundThe highest score you reached in a single round.
KillsThe number of worms you have taken out.
FlagsThe number of flags you brought to the centre. This sets the difficulty of your next flag.
Play timeHow long you have played in total.
Last playedDate and time of your last round.
Unlocked itemsWhich upgrades, skins and grounds you unlocked using in-game points.
ClanWhich clan you are in, your headshots for that clan, and whether you want to receive invitations.

If you founded a clan, we also store the clan name and its members. The legal basis for everything in this section is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Publicly visible

Your player name, headshots, score, clan tag and crown position are visible to other players. That is the purpose of a leaderboard. Your e-mail address is not visible to other players. Choose a player name that does not identify you personally if you do not want it to.

6. Clan notifications

The server sends predefined notifications to clan members, for example when someone joins or leaves a clan. No data beyond that listed in section 5 is processed for this.

7. Data stored on your device

Settings such as language, the position and size of the on-screen buttons and your chosen appearance are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. They are removed when you uninstall the game.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as you use the game. If you have not played for six months, we send a reminder to the e-mail address linked to the account. If you do not play within 14 days after that, we delete the account together with its leaderboard entry and e-mail address. You can delete your account yourself at any time before that: see Delete account.

9. Sharing

We do not sell data. Data is only passed to the providers named above (GitHub for this website, Oracle Cloud for the game server, Google for the sign-in) to the extent described, and where we are legally obliged to do so.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). An e-mail to the address given above is enough; please include the e-mail address of your Google account so we can find your account.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; the competent authority is the one for your place of residence.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are younger, only create an account with your parents' consent.

12. Changes

If the game changes, we will update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
