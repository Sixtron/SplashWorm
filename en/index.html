What to expect

Phone and PC together

Players on Windows PCs and players on phones share the same world on the same server. No separate rounds.

Collecting flags

Four flags stand at the edge of the map. Take one and bring it to the blue circle in the centre – while the bots come hunting for you. Every flag makes the next one harder, and after the 20th flag you get a second life, after the 40th a third.

Clans

Found a clan and invite your friends. The clan tag appears above your name. Only headshots on members of other clans count for your clan.

Leaderboard and shop

Whoever has the most headshots leads the leaderboard; the top ten wear a crown. Points you earn are spent in the shop on appearance, grounds and stronger abilities. You never need real money.

Getting started

The guide covers everything: controls, points, the holes in the ground and how the flags work.
