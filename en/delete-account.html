This page belongs to the game SplashWorm (Android and Windows), offered by Dietmar Imberg. It explains how to have your player account and all data stored for it deleted.

Option 1: In the game

The fastest way. Open the menu in the game and choose “Delete account”. For safety a confirmation code is shown which you have to type in – so nobody can delete your account by accident. After that it is deleted immediately. Nothing else is needed from you.

Option 2: By e-mail

You do not need the game installed for this. Send an e-mail to:

splashworm@online.de

Subject: Delete account
In the message, state the e-mail address of the Google account you played with. That is the only way we can identify your account.

We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm the deletion by reply. There is no charge and you do not have to give a reason.

What gets deleted

The complete record of your account on our game server, that is:

  • your Google account identifier and your e-mail address,
  • your player name,
  • headshots, score, best round, kills, flags,
  • play time and the time of your last round,
  • all upgrades, skins and grounds unlocked with in-game points,
  • your clan membership and your headshots for that clan,
  • your entry in the public leaderboard.

Your account is then gone completely. We keep no copy for further use and nothing is passed to third parties. If server backups exist at the time of deletion, your record may still be contained in them for up to 30 days until those backups are overwritten.

What else happens

  • Data on your device (language, position and size of the on-screen buttons, chosen appearance) is only stored there and never with us. It is removed when you uninstall the game.
  • If you founded a clan, the clan is not dissolved: the best member becomes the new leader.
  • If you play again later, signing in creates a new, empty account. Points, flags and unlocked items cannot be restored.

Nothing to refund

SplashWorm is free. There are no purchases with real money, so we hold no payment data and no invoices that we would have to keep.

Changing individual details instead

If you do not want to delete everything, you can also just change your player name or leave your clan – both directly in the game. Further rights such as access and rectification are described in the privacy policy.
