The idea

You control a worm. The more you collect, the longer you grow and the more points you score. If your head touches another worm, you are out and start again. If another worm runs into you, it falls apart – and you can absorb its points off the ground before anyone else does.

Players on Windows PCs and players on phones share the same world on the same server. There are no separate rounds.

Controls

You steer the worm towards where you point – with your finger on a phone, with the mouse on a PC. On top of that you have four buttons:

MagnetPulls pieces on the ground towards you, including ones further away.
GhostMakes you briefly pass through things, so you can get out of a tight spot.
TurboBoost. You move faster for a short time.
StopBrakes and holds you in place.

Magnet, Ghost and Stop each work for a limited time and then need a pause before you can use them again. Both can be improved in the shop.

Adjusting the buttons

  • Phone: In the settings you can freely move each of the four buttons and change its size.
  • PC: In the settings you can change the key assignment.

Points

  • Collecting: Every piece on the ground makes you longer and scores points.
  • Absorbing: When a worm falls apart, its points stay on the ground. Whoever gets there first takes them.
  • Headshots: Hitting another worm exactly on the head gives a large bonus on top.
  • Special pickups: Some pieces on the ground are worth considerably more than the normal ones.

Holes in the ground

There are holes in the world. They open and close again.

  • A hole is only dangerous while it is fully open.
  • If your head falls into a fully open hole, you are out.
  • The hole you came out of yourself also becomes a trap after a short while. So move away promptly.

Collecting flags

Four flags stand at the edge of the map, at the 3, 6, 9 and 12 o'clock positions. In the centre of the map there is a blue circle.

  1. Drive into the hole in the centre of the map. It beams you outwards, to the edge – to where the flags are.
  2. Drive to a flag and touch it. You are then carrying it.
  3. Bring it to the blue circle in the centre and drive into it. The flag is then delivered.
  4. As soon as you are carrying a flag, the bots come hunting for you.

With every flag you deliver, the next one gets harder – and more valuable.

In the flag game you basically have only 1 life, and the ghost only once per round, for 5 seconds.

In flag mode you have only one life, and Ghost is available just once, for 5 seconds.

Extra lives

10 flagsYou get a second life, permanently.
20 flagsYou get a third life, permanently.

(Subject to change in future versions of the game)

Clans

You can join a clan at any time. Founding your own takes 10 hours of play time.

  • You can found a clan or join one. The clan tag then appears above your name, so everyone can see who you belong to.
  • Only headshots on members of other clans count for your clan. Players without a clan and your own clan mates score nothing.
  • The server sends you notifications, for example when someone joins or leaves the clan.
  • You can switch invitations off entirely in the settings.
  • You can also play without your clan. Then no tag appears above your name and your hits count for no clan.

Leaderboard and crowns

The leaderboard is led by whoever has the most headshots. The top ten wear a crown above their head in the game, so everyone can see who they are up against.

If you have not played for seven days, you are no longer shown to other players in the leaderboard. Your result stays saved and is back immediately once you play again.

Shop

Points you earn in the game can be spent in the shop. You never need real money – there is nothing to buy anywhere in the game.

  • Upgrades for Magnet, Ghost and Stop: stronger, longer lasting and with a shorter pause.
  • Appearance for your worm.
  • Grounds, the surface you play on: grass, earth, water, sand, lava, ice and more.

Account

If you sign in with Google, your name, points, clan and everything you unlocked are kept, even if you change device or switch between phone and PC. Which data is stored is described in the privacy policy.
